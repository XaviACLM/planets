To mention in the info modal: These are just the standard lots in modern astrological practice (cf. Chris Brennan, Robert Hand, Rober Schmidt). All from DS and PA. No conflicts except Eros, where we choose DS because it's more widely used. Wealth (DS) is actually called livelihood, but wealthy is more widely used.

DS, PA = Dorotheus of Sidon, Paulus of Alexandria

Formulas are straightforward, except that the signs on the last two terms flip on nocturnal charts. Easy enough.

That 2nd / 8th / etc stuff: refers to house cusps, obvi ruler 2nd being the ruler of the 2nd cusp. A bit annoying, definitely, but we'll manage - nodePositions now depends on houseCuspPositions (no circular dependencies, so all is good). We'll want a method NodePositions.changeHouseCuspPositions. It's probably fine to NOT keep an internal NodePositions._houseCuspPositions - the semantic dependence really isn't very strong, once NodePositions knows where the lots are then we're all good.

It does introduce a problem though which is that we can't cleanly define Lots as a triplet of Nodes, since house cusps aren't nodes, much less house cusp rulers. We could have a relatively complicated data model to work with this but... eh. We can just stick it all in a big function for now. Proper data model may become relevant later if we offer full arabic lot support. Oh yeah and remember they have self-dependencies, e.g. many depend on fortune or spirit
