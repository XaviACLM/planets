- Obviously the name is horrible and we need a better one.

- The create method, and also the constructor, will need to take these dependencies as args (HCP needs ZSP, NP needs HCP and ZSP). However HCP does not keep a reference to ZSP because... well, truthfully, for no good reason, it just vaguely feels to me like this would be incorrect.
